ABOUT THE STUDY PACK

A companion to your course.

This English study pack covers six non-programming core subjects and two electives. It combines explanations, worked examples, concept checks and written practice based on the accessible supplied course files.

The original source audit records file-level coverage, including scanned and image-only material that could not be fully extracted. Refer to your lecturer’s current course outline for examinable scope and conventions.

Read completion is self-marked. MCQ performance records your latest submitted answer to each question. Written practice and self-checks do not add to that score.
